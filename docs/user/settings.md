# Settings

Personalize Projekt-Planer: display name, profile picture, theme, security and account management.

**Note:** Account settings are only available for signed-in users. Without an account, only display options are available.

## Account

- **Display Name:** Change the name shown to other users in cloud projects.
- **Profile Picture:** Upload a picture or remove the current one.

Click **Save** to apply changes.

## Display

The **Display** section contains:

### Theme

| Option     | Description                                          |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **System** | Automatically follows system preference (light/dark) |
| **Light**  | Always light theme                                   |
| **Dark**   | Always dark theme                                    |

### Row height

Choose the default row height: **Comfortable**, **Compact** or **Dense**. This is the starting value for new projects. See [Zoom & Density](zoom-density.md) for details.

## Security

### Change email

Enter the new address under **New Email Address** and click **Update Email**. A confirmation link is sent to the new address.

### Change password

1.  Enter your **current password**.
2.  Enter the **new password** (minimum 6 characters).
3.  Repeat the new password.
4.  Click **Update Password**.

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA protects your account with an additional code from an authenticator app (e.g. Google Authenticator or Authy).

1.  Click **Enable 2FA**.
2.  Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
3.  Enter the 6-digit code and confirm.

When 2FA is active, you'll be prompted for the code on every login. To disable it, click **Disable 2FA** and confirm with your password.

### Delete account

Deleting your account is irreversible. All cloud projects where you are the owner are also deleted.

**Warning:** Deleting your account cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed. Type **DELETE** to confirm.

## Sign in / Sign out

Click **Sign In** in the top right to create an account or sign in. Via the account menu you can sign out with **Sign Out**. After signing out, only local projects remain accessible.

## Forgot password

Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen. Enter your email and click **Send reset link**. You'll receive an email with a link to reset your password.
